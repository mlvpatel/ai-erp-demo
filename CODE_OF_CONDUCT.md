# Code of conduct

AI ERP Demo is intended to become a serious open-source ERP project. ERP work
touches money, inventory, people, permissions, and regulated operations, so the
community standard is simple: be precise, respectful, and safety-minded.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to contributors with different experience levels.
- Assume good intent, but verify claims with code, tests, docs, or reproducible
  examples.
- Keep discussion focused on the product, architecture, user impact, and risk.
- Disagree clearly without attacking people.
- Protect private information. Do not post customer data, credentials,
  production exports, or sensitive prompts.
- Treat security and AI-safety issues with urgency and discretion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers redirect them.
- Encouraging unsafe ERP behavior, such as bypassing approvals for money, stock,
  payroll, permissions, or compliance actions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block users, or restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately using `SECURITY.md`, not
through public issues.
